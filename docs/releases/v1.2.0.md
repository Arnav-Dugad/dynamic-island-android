## Island 1.2.0: blends into One UI

![Island 1.2 on a Galaxy S23+](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Arnav-Dugad/dynamic-island-android/v1.2.0/docs/images/v1.2-on-device.png)

### Which file do I download?

| File | For | What works |
|---|---|---|
| **Island-Lite-v1.2.0.apk** | Friends installing from a browser | Charging, battery, Bluetooth, timers & stopwatch, flashlight, calendar, system events, Glance, local API |
| **Island-v1.2.0.apk** | The full experience | Everything above plus music, calls, navigation, deliveries & rides, downloads and notifications |

Both editions install over each other and over earlier versions, and keep your settings. If Android blocks the full edition from a browser, install it over USB (`adb install -r Island-v1.2.0.apk`) or start with Lite.

### Blend with One UI

A new screen that makes the island behave like it came with the phone. The status bar and pop-up options need a one-time grant from a computer (`adb shell pm grant com.arnav.island android.permission.WRITE_SECURE_SETTINGS`). Island never grants this to itself, and turning an option off puts back exactly what was there before.

- **Seamless status bar**: while a banner or card covers the status bar, the clock, battery and signal icons step aside, then return as it closes. Camera, microphone and location indicators are never hidden.
- **Replace One UI pop-ups** (off by default): each notification appears once, in the island. One UI's pop-ups come back by themselves whenever the island is hidden, the screen is locked or Island stops. Turn it off before uninstalling Island.
- **Follows the system**: motion speeds up or slows down with your animation scale, haptics follow the touch-vibration setting, and island sounds follow Touch sounds.
- **Unlock bloom**: a ring in your wallpaper's accent colour around the camera when you unlock.
- **One UI look** throughout the app: a tall header with a centred title that folds into the toolbar, and One UI switches.
- Direct links to the One UI settings Island can't change for you (notification icons, pop-up style).

### New live activities
- **Deliveries & rides**: Swiggy, Zomato, Uber, Blinkit, Zepto, Ola, Rapido and more, plus any app using Android 16 Live Updates. The pill shows the ETA; the card shows the route with stops and the courier moving along it.
- **Next meeting** (optional): a countdown before events in your calendars, with a Join button for Meet, Zoom and Teams links. Asks for calendar access only when you turn it on.
- **Flashlight**: a glowing torch in the pill and a brightness slider in the card, using your flash's own brightness steps.
- **Screenshot preview** (optional, off by default): the newest screenshot drops into the island with Share and Edit.
- **Charge limit coach** (optional): one gentle "unplug now" when charging passes your limit. It stays quiet if One UI's battery protection has already paused charging.

### Motion and detail
- **Grab and flick**: long-press an open card and it lifts, follows your finger and springs home.
- **Waveform morph**: pause and the equalizer flows round into a progress ring.
- **Liquid split**: the second activity pinches off the pill with surface tension.
- **Stopwatch lap dial**: a tick for every lap and a ripple when you take one.
- **Reply sent**: after a quick reply, the island shows it going out, then a check.
- **Live line**: when a player publishes changing text (synced lyrics, radio text), it rolls in under the artist.

### App
- **Your own theme**: pick the rim, glow and highlight colours.
- **Per-app motion**: give an app its own spring personality (for example Snappy in games).
- **Gallery**: every kind of activity playing live, ready to pin to the island.
- **What's new** is now a timeline of every version, with replayable demos.
- **Share your setup**: a QR code or link that gives a friend your island size, motion, theme and behaviour. Calibration and anything personal stay on your phone.
- **Diagnostics**: a local report (device, island state, recent warnings, last crash) to copy into a bug report. Nothing is ever sent.

### Privacy

Still no internet permission, no accounts, no analytics and no ads. Calendar and photo access are optional, asked for only when you turn those features on, and never stored.
