## Island 1.1.0

![New in 1.1 on a Galaxy S23+](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Arnav-Dugad/dynamic-island-android/v1.1.0/docs/images/v1.1-on-device.png)

### Which file do I download?

| File | For | What works |
|---|---|---|
| **Island-Lite-v1.1.0.apk** | Friends installing from a browser | Charging, battery, Bluetooth, timers & stopwatch, system events, Glance, local API |
| **Island-v1.1.0.apk** | The full experience | Everything above plus music, calls, navigation, downloads and notifications |

Both are the same app with the same signature, so either one installs over the other and keeps your settings.
Android may block the full edition when it is installed from a browser ("App blocked to protect your device").
That is Play Protect's enhanced fraud protection, which applies to any sideloaded app that asks for notification access.
Install the full edition over USB instead (`adb install -r Island-v1.1.0.apk`), or start with Lite.

Updating from 1.0: install the new APK over the old one. Settings are kept.

### New

**Motion**
- **Icon flight**: a new notification's icon flies in from the status bar along a spring arc, then opens into the banner. Album art and caller photos glide between the pill and the card.
- **Squash & stretch**: the island stretches with its own speed and settles like something physical. Corners react to speed and drags snap to magnetic detents.
- **Throw to dismiss**: fling a banner sideways and it flies off at the speed of your swipe.
- **Rolling numbers**: timer, stopwatch and charging digits roll like an odometer.
- **Arrival pulse, lens glint, blur-in text and tilt depth**, each with its own switch.
- **Paired haptics** on Android's haptic primitives, plus optional synthesized sound design (off by default, silent in vibrate or silent mode).

**Activities**
- **Media card**: up next (tap to play it), output device and a volume slider, with a soft glow in the artwork's colours.
- **Group chats**: stacked avatars for the people in a conversation.
- **Quick reply**: reply from the expanded notification using the app's own reply action, with its suggested replies.
- **Per-app accent colours**, taken from the app or picked in Apps.
- **Timer final countdown**: the last ten seconds pulse and warm to red, with a haptic tick each second.
- **Charging graph**: live measured charging power in the expanded card, and "Paused at 85%" when One UI's battery protection holds the charge.
- **Glance**: long-press the empty island for the date, battery, next alarm, timers and what's playing.
- **Stack peek**: pull an open card further down to see every running activity.

**App**
- **Island Studio**: drag the edges of a blueprint and the real island resizes live.
- **Motion lab**: the expand and collapse springs drawn as curves, updating as you move the sliders.
- **Animated theme gallery**.
- **What's new**: plays each feature on the live preview (opens once after updating).
- **Share with friends**: a QR code, share link and a step-by-step install guide.
- **Check for updates** and one-tap **Obtainium** setup for automatic updates. Island itself still has no internet permission.
- **Status bar cleanup** (experimental): hides status bar icons the island already shows. It needs a one-time `adb shell pm grant com.arnav.island android.permission.WRITE_SECURE_SETTINGS`, and your previous icon settings are restored when you turn it off.

**Builds**
- Releases are now built, tested, signed and published by GitHub Actions. `SHA256SUMS.txt` is attached.

### Privacy

Still no internet permission, no accounts, no analytics and no ads. Quick replies go straight to the app that asked for them, and nothing is stored.
